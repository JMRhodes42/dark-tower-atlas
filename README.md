# Atlas of the Dark Tower

An interactive map of Stephen King's universe with the Dark Tower at the center.

- **Explore**: every Tower-connected book and major adaptation, placed by how close it sits to the Tower.
- **Threads**: follow the Man in Black, the Crimson King, the Breakers, Father Callahan, the Turtle, Derry, doors between worlds, and Flanagan's adaptations.
- **Your path**: pick what you've read or watched and get a short route to the Tower.
- **Spoilers**: off by default.

Live at https://jmrhodes42.github.io/dark-tower-atlas/

A fan project. Not affiliated with Stephen King, his publishers, Amazon, or Mike Flanagan. No cover art, stills, or character likenesses are used.

Everything lives in `index.html`. No build step.
